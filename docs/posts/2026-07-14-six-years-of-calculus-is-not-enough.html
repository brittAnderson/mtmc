Six Years of Advanced Calculus is not Enough to be an Academic Psychologist

Posted on July 14, 2026 by Britt Anderson

Don Norman on the Cognition Project …

… reports that when beginning graduate school Duncan Luce told him “[you] don’t know enough mathematics.” This was despite “six years of advanced calculus. Those are interesting days.”

But apparently cognitive psychology has gotten easier since Norman’s time as many programs don’t require their undergraduates to take any calculus.
