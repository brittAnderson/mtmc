Psychology is Harder than Physics

Posted on July 14, 2026 by Britt Anderson

“Imagine how hard physics would be if electrons could think.”1

From this we might reasonably conclude that psychology is a more difficult science than physics, at least as regards developing empirically robust theories. If so, why do so many people in psychology think we need to know less math than physicists?
