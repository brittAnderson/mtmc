Grant Sanderson on Category Theory

Posted on September 29, 2026 by Britt Anderson

Making Connections

And so one of the shifts in math over the last century is one towards thinking in terms of something called categories. And that if you try to ask what new problems do categories solve to a mathematician, they might be able to find some. But you'll actually struggle to get good answers on what's the open problem that is solved because of our understanding of category theory. Instead, it's more like that was an entire way of reframing how people think that provided a cleaner understanding of lots of different stuff and it made it more swift for their brains to go from one field to another and recognize connections.
