Cognitive Science is a Hodge-Podge

Posted on August 27, 2026 by Britt Anderson

From the Preface

In colloquial terms, cognitive science in its present state mostly amounts to a hodge-podge of theories, model, and empirical results that lack genuine predictive and explanatory power, and a common solid basis for unification.1

  1. Ronaldo Vigo, Mathematical Principles of Human Conceptual Behavior (Psychology Press, 2014), doi:10.4324/9781315882772.↩︎
