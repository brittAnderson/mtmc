# Category Theory is for Theory Building

> The natural role, let us at least say, the original role of category theory, is theory building, right? That's how it came into being ... that's why Eilenberg and MacLane introduced it and that is what it is best at. It's for theory building right? Whenever you have a question that is not solve this equation in a well-defined context ... but where the question is a meta-question like what is the actual theory we're looking for ...

— Urs Schreiber on the *About Logic Podcast* [#12](https://aboutlogic.podigee.io/12-neue-episode), 20 May 2026
