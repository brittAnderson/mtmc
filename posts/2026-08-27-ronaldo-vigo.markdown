# From the Preface

> In colloquial terms, cognitive science in its present state mostly
> amounts to a hodge-podge of theories, model, and empirical results
> that lack genuine predictive and explanatory power, and a common solid
> basis for unification.[^1]

[^1]: Ronaldo Vigo, *Mathematical Principles of Human Conceptual
    Behavior* (Psychology Press, 2014),
    doi:[10.4324/9781315882772](https://doi.org/10.4324/9781315882772).
