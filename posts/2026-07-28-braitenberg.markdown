# Point #2

> We are convinced that ultimately a satisfactory explanation of thought
> and behaviour will be given in a language akin to that of physics,
> i.e. in mathematical terms[^1]

[^1]: Valentino Braitenberg, "Manifesto of Brain Science," in
    *Information Processing in the Cortex* (Springer Berlin Heidelberg,
    1992), 473--77,
    doi:[10.1007/978-3-642-49967-8_29](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-49967-8_29).
